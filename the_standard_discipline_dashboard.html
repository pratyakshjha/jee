Operating code

Rules for the person you are becoming.

1. I do what I said I would do.

2. I do not negotiate with distractions.

3. A bad day does not become a bad week.

4. When plans fail, I adapt instead of quitting.

5. I start without waiting for motivation.

6. Important work comes before entertainment.

7. I protect sleep, health and recovery.

8. Other people's behaviour does not control my standards.

9. I review failures without excuses or self-attack.

10. I collect proof that I can trust myself.

Definition of hard: consistent under pressure, honest about failure, willing to do difficult work, and able to recover quickly. Not reckless, sleep-deprived, injured, or miserable for its own sake.
